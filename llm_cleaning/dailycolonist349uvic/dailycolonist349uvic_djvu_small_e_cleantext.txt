The Case of the English naval Lieutenant.— Col. Leonard, U. S. A., held an examination, at San Francisco, on Thursday, in the presence of United States Marshal Hand and Chief Burke, into the case of Lieut. Newport, of the Royal Navy, recently arrested for uttering treasonable language towards the United States Government. During the examination it was ascertained that he is a half-pay officer of the British service, and had until recently been employed in charge of the British mails on the overland route from Alexandria to India. While on this service he suffered sunstroke, and ever since has been more or less light-headed, and his disorder is greatly aggravated if he drinks at all.
