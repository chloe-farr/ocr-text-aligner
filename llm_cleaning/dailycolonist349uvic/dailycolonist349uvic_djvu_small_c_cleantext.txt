Washington, Oct. 18. — A dispatch from 
headquarters of the army of the Potomac 
says the reconnoisances under Hancock 
and Humphrey, returned after going within 
five miles of Bunker Hill. The enemy did not 
interfere with the return of Hancock, but fol- 
lowed Humphrey with artillery and cavalry 
to within a short distance of Shepherdstown, 
opening on him with artillery wherever they 
could. Our loss was one killed and six wound- 
ed. The troops crossed the Potomac safely 
last night at Shepherdstown with a number  of 
prisoners. One hundred rebel wounded left 
at Charlestown were brought within our lines 
and will be paroled. The rebels in disputing 
Humphrey had 30 or 40 killed.
